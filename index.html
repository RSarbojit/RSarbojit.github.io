About

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at the Institut de Mathématiques de Marseille (I2M), Aix-Marseille Université, in the Groupe Mathématiques de l’Aléatoire, working with Professor Anna Dudek.

I develop statistical models for analysing non-stationary biological signals. Before Marseille, I was a postdoc in the Biostatistics Group at KAUST with Professor Hernando Ombao, where I worked on classification and dependence analysis of high-dimensional time series, with applications to neuroimaging. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from IIT Kanpur, with a thesis on distance-based classifiers for high-dimensional data.
